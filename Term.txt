1.a transformer is composed of attention heads and MLPs, with each one performing operations on the residual stream
2.the attention heads in a single layer operate independently, and that they have the role of calculating attention patterns (which determine where information is moved to & from in the residual stream)
3.Transformer modules :
1) LayerNorm (transforming the input to have zero mean and unit variance)
2) Positional embedding (a lookup table from position indices to residual stream vectors)
3) Attention (the method of computing attention patterns for residual stream vectors)
4)MLP (the collection of linear and nonlinear transformations which operate on each residual stream vector in the same way)
5)Embedding (a lookup table from tokens to residual stream vectors)
6)Unembedding (a matrix for converting residual stream vectors into a distribution over tokens)
